# Scoring v2

The v2 engine is deliberately deterministic and evidence-first.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | What earns credit | What does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | Built, designed, owned, led, deployed, migrated | Repeated generic verbs with no substance |
| Technical depth | Distinct stack + systems/architecture concepts | Repeating the same technology |
| Business impact | Quantified outcomes and causal relationships | Years, dates, or arbitrary numbers |
| Evidence quality | Combined ownership, technical depth, and impact | Keyword density alone |
| Role alignment | Terms genuinely supported by the profile | Keyword stuffing |
| Seniority | Explicit level + relevant scope | Treating technical verbs as titles |

## Anti-gaming rules

- Metrics exclude plain years such as `2021` and `2023`.
- Technical scoring counts distinct signals rather than repeated mentions.
- Ownership scoring is capped.
- Impact gets stronger credit when an outcome is causally linked to the work.
- Rewrite recommendations never manufacture metrics, scale, employers, technologies, or outcomes.

## Rewrite philosophy

The optimizer can identify missing evidence and safely improve passive framing, but it does not invent achievements. A missing metric is reported as a gap that the user must substantiate.
